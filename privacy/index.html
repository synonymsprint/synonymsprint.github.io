Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Synonym Sprint is a word game. It's built to collect as little about you as possible.

What stays on your phone

Everything the app saves stays on your device:

  • Your scores, stats, streaks and Daily Word results
  • SAT & ACT study progress
  • Words you save to My Words and words you suggest
  • Your chosen name, color theme and settings

Synonym Sprint has no accounts of its own and no database. We can't see any of this, and it's deleted if you delete the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

During a round, the app listens through your microphone so it can hear the words you say.

  • Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. Apple may process the audio on its servers to do this, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • The app only uses the text to check your answers. It doesn't save or share your audio or the text.
  • The microphone is only on while a round is being played.

You can type your answers instead, and you can turn off microphone access any time in Settings → Synonym Sprint.

Game Center (leaderboards and online races)

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, the app posts your scores to Game Center leaderboards, and online Head-to-Head races are run through Game Center. Other players can see your Game Center nickname and scores. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can sign out or change what others see in Settings → Game Center.

Nearby races

Head-to-Head with a friend in the same room uses Apple's peer-to-peer networking to connect directly to their phone. Only your chosen name and your race scores and words are sent, and only to that phone.

Camera (Party Mode)

Party Mode is off unless you turn it on. When it's on, the app records the screen, sound and your front camera during a round so you can make a video clip. The clip stays on your phone unless you choose to save or share it.

Sharing and challenge links

When you tap Share or Challenge a friend, the app creates a message. It's only sent if you choose where to send it. Challenge links contain the word, your score, your chosen name and the words you found, so the person you send it to can play the same round.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no ads, no tracking and no cookies of its own. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Game Center and its settings for children are managed by Apple and by parents through Screen Time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
